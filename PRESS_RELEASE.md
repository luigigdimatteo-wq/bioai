# 📰 FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

**BioAI Therapeutics Unveils Cloud-Native AI Platform to Slash Preclinical Drug Discovery Timelines from Months to Days**

*Enterprise-grade SaaS suite combines real-time 3D conformational docking, toxicity profiling, and De Novo generative lead discovery for mid-sized biotechs and CROs.*

**SAN FRANCISCO & LONDON — October 2026** — BioAI Therapeutics, an emerging leader in computational biopharma software, today announced the global launch of its unified Bio-AI Drug Discovery Platform. Engineered to address the $2.6 billion bottleneck and 90%+ failure rate in early-stage drug development, BioAI provides computational chemistry teams with an end-to-end, browser-accessible suite for high-throughput screening and novel lead generation.

### Revolutionizing In Silico Molecular Intelligence
While legacy drug discovery software remains siloed, complex, and computationally heavy, BioAI Therapeutics delivers a cloud-native microservices architecture designed for immediate deployment:
* **Interactive 3D Molecular Conformations**: Real-time spatial visualization powered by py3Dmol.
* **Predictive Toxicity & Structural Alerts**: Automated screening for PAINS and genotoxic toxicophores.
* **Massive Parallel Batch Screening**: High-throughput ADMET profiling across thousands of chemical candidates simultaneously.
* **De Novo Generative Discovery**: Stochastic AI engine synthesizing patentable novel scaffolds for oncology, CNS, and antiviral indications.
* **Validated Precision**: Achieved a 94.2% accuracy benchmark against standard ChEMBL/PubChem reference datasets.

"Traditional drug discovery is constrained by linear, costly trial-and-error cycles," said **Luigi Di Matteo**, Founder & CEO of BioAI Therapeutics. "Our mission at BioAI is to democratize molecular intelligence—giving biopharma teams instant, enterprise-grade tools to eliminate toxic candidates early and discover life-saving therapeutics at unprecedented speed."

### Pre-Seed Funding & Expansion
Alongside the product launch, BioAI Therapeutics has opened its **$1.5 Million Pre-Seed funding round** to scale its containerized cloud backend, expand university research partnerships, and accelerate *in vitro* synthesis validation of top AI-generated lead candidates.
